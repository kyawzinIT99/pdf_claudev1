## Findings and resolution

### 1. Our Work entry — improved

The previous hero was visually large but abstract. It did not show real community activity and the oversized title delayed the page's meaning. The new hero uses a different authentic activity photograph, a readable editorial title and a direct explanation of faith, culture and practical connection.

### 2. Permanent work areas — improved

The three areas now use real community photographs. The legacy Community care card is upgraded to Emergency relief and care when its old default wording is encountered. The page remains an evergreen explanation, not a daily feed.

### 3. Current updates — clearly separated

Our Work now ends with one clear route to News and Stories and the official Facebook group. Published posts are fetched only for News and Stories; they no longer create empty or duplicate feed blocks on Our Work.

### 4. Flood fundraising — publication blocked pending verification

The public Facebook group was accessible and showed a visible Perth-based community with 2.2K members and recent media. The current captured feed did not provide evidence of an Ayeyarwady flood appeal, authorised beneficiary or collection controls. A specific appeal should be published only in News and Stories after the organisation confirms its date, beneficiary, collection method, closing date, approval and reporting commitment.

## Accessibility and limits

- The new hero photograph has descriptive alternative text.
- Main navigation, News and Stories route and Facebook link are keyboard-reachable links.
- Browser console showed no warnings or errors in reviewed states.
- Screenshot review cannot prove full keyboard order, screen-reader output or publication consent for people shown in photographs.

final result: passed
