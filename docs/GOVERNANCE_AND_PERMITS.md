# Government, Land, and Permit Readiness

This checklist protects the organisation’s reputation before the website states or implies that a government body has approved, funded, leased, partnered with, or endorsed a project.

It is an operational checklist, not legal advice. The responsible Australian authority and a qualified adviser should confirm the exact requirements for the organisation, location, activity, and jurisdiction.

## Evidence required before a public claim

- Legal organisation name, registration details, responsible officers, and current contact address
- Written land ownership, lease, licence, or occupancy authority covering the relevant area
- Planning, building, event, fundraising, food, child-safety, environmental, or activity permits that apply
- Public liability, volunteer, workers compensation, property, and activity-specific insurance as required
- Working with Children Checks, safeguarding policies, and consent records where children or vulnerable people are involved
- Written grant, partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement terms
- Approved name, logo, acknowledgement, disclaimer, and publication wording from the relevant authority
- Start date, expiry date, geographic scope, conditions, and renewal owner for each permission
- Copyright, photography, privacy, and community-story consent
- A named internal reviewer and final approver for every government-related public statement

## Publishing control

1. Upload supporting evidence to restricted organisational storage.
2. Record the authority, reference number, scope, dates, and responsible owner.
3. Draft the public statement using only the written approved wording.
4. Obtain legal/governance review when the claim could imply endorsement or official status.
5. Publish only after an authorised staff member approves it.
6. Set a review or automatic unpublish date before the permission expires.
7. Keep the approval and published version in the audit history.

## Brand boundary

The COMMON KIND mark uses an original Australia-inspired palette and community motif. Do not combine it with the Commonwealth Coat of Arms, Australian Government Design System branding, an agency logo, a council logo, or official endorsement language without written permission.

Suggested neutral wording:

> We acknowledge the support of [authority] for [specific project], under [agreement or program], for the period [dates].

Use that wording only when the evidence and agreement support it.
